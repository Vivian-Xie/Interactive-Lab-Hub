Goose Counting Game

Game Rules:

  • The image will show for 3 seconds - count the geese quickly
  • After the image disappears, wait for the 5-second countdown
  • When countdown finishes, you have 5 seconds to press your button
  • Press button each time you count a goose
  • Hold button for 2 seconds to submit your answer
  • The fastest correct answer wins
